| Ingredient              | Amount | Unit    | Note           |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ------- | -------------- |
| Dried pasta             | 200    | g       |                |
| Olive oil               | 2      | tbsp    |                |
| Small onion             | 1      |         | Finely chopped |
| Garlic                  | 2      | cloves  | Minced         |
| Canned chopped tomatoes | 400    | g       |                |
| Dried oregano           | 1      | tsp     |                |
| Salt and black pepper   |        |         | To taste       |
| Fresh basil leaves      | 1      | handful |                |
| Grated Parmesan         |        |         | To serve       |

Prep time 10 minutes. Cook time 20 minutes.

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, until soft.
3. Stir in the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
4. Add the tomatoes and oregano. Simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in salted water according to the package instructions.
6. Reserve a mug of pasta water, then drain the pasta.
7. Toss the pasta with the sauce. Add a splash of pasta water if needed to loosen it.
8. Season with salt and pepper. Serve with basil and Parmesan.
