| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ripe pears | 4 |  | Halved and cored |
| Maple syrup | 3 | tbsp |  |
| Neutral oil | 1 | tbsp |  |
| Ground cinnamon | 1 | tsp |  |
| Walnuts | 40 | g | Chopped |
| Water | 3 | tbsp |  |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Put the pear halves cut-side up in a baking dish.
2. Mix the maple syrup, oil, and cinnamon. Spoon over the pears and pour the water into the dish.
3. Roast for 25 minutes, spooning the juices over the pears halfway through.
4. Scatter over the walnuts and roast for another 5 minutes, until the pears are tender.
