| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frozen peas | 500 | g |  |
| Onion | 1 |  | Diced |
| Olive oil | 1 | tbsp |  |
| Vegetable stock | 700 | ml | Gluten-free |
| Fresh mint | 10 | g | Leaves only |
| Lemon juice | 1 | tbsp |  |
| Black pepper |  |  | To taste |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Cook the onion for 5 minutes.
2. Add the stock and bring to a boil. Add the peas and simmer for 5 minutes.
3. Remove from the heat and add the mint and lemon juice.
4. Blend with an immersion blender until smooth. Season with pepper and serve.
