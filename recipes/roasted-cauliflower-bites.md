| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cauliflower | 1 |  | Cut into small florets |
| Olive oil | 2 | tbsp |  |
| Smoked paprika | 1 | tsp |  |
| Ground cumin | 0.5 | tsp |  |
| Salt | 0.5 | tsp |  |
| Lemon juice | 1 | tbsp |  |
| Fresh parsley | 1 | tbsp | Chopped |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Toss the cauliflower with the oil, paprika, cumin, and salt.
2. Spread the florets in a single layer on a baking tray.
3. Roast for 25 minutes, turning once, until browned and tender.
4. Toss with the lemon juice and parsley. Serve warm.
