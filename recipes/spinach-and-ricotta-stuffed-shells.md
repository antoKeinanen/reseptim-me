| Salt | 0.5 | tsp |  |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Boil the pasta shells for 2 minutes less than the package cooking time, then drain.
2. Mix the spinach, ricotta, oregano, and salt in a bowl.
3. Spread the passata in a baking dish. Fill each shell with the spinach mixture and place in the sauce.
4. Scatter the mozzarella over the shells. Cover with foil and bake for 20 minutes.
5. Remove the foil and bake for another 10 minutes, until the sauce bubbles and the cheese melts.
