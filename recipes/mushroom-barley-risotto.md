| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pearl barley | 250 | g |  |
| Mushrooms | 400 | g | Sliced |
| Onion | 1 |  | Diced |
| Garlic | 2 | cloves | Minced |
| Olive oil | 2 | tbsp |  |
| Vegetable stock | 1 | l | Hot |
| Fresh parsley | 2 | tbsp | Chopped |
| Black pepper |  |  | To taste |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a deep pan over medium heat. Cook the onion and mushrooms for 8 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and barley. Stir for 1 minute.
3. Add the hot stock a ladle at a time. Stir often and add more stock as the barley absorbs it.
4. Cook for 35 to 45 minutes until the barley is tender. Add a little hot water if needed.
5. Stir in the parsley and season with pepper.
