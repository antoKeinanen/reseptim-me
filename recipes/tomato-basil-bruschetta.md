| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Baguette | 1 |  | Cut into 12 slices, vegan |
| Tomatoes | 400 | g | Diced |
| Fresh basil | 10 | g | Torn |
| Garlic | 1 | clove | Halved |
| Olive oil | 2 | tbsp |  |
| Balsamic vinegar | 1 | tbsp |  |
| Salt |  |  | To taste |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Arrange the bread on a tray and toast for 6 to 8 minutes.
2. Rub the warm bread with the cut sides of the garlic.
3. Mix the tomatoes, basil, oil, and vinegar. Season with salt.
4. Spoon the tomatoes onto the toast just before serving.
