| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mixed olives | 300 | g | Pitted and drained |
| Olive oil | 3 | tbsp |  |
| Lemon | 1 |  | Zest only |
| Garlic | 1 | clove | Thinly sliced |
| Fresh rosemary | 1 | sprig |  |
| Chili flakes | 0.25 | tsp |  |

## Instructions

1. Warm the oil, lemon zest, garlic, rosemary, and chili flakes in a small saucepan over low heat for 3 minutes.
2. Add the olives and warm for 2 minutes without frying them.
3. Transfer to a bowl and let stand for 20 minutes before serving.
