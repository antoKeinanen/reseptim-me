## Instructions

1. Cook the noodles according to the package instructions, then drain.
2. Whisk the peanut butter, soy sauce, lime juice, water, and maple syrup in a bowl.
3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook the tofu for 8 minutes, turning until golden.
4. Add the carrots and cook for 3 minutes.
5. Add the noodles and sauce. Toss over the heat for 2 minutes and serve.
