| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Potatoes | 900 | g | Peeled and thinly sliced |
| Leeks | 2 |  | Washed and sliced |
| Butter | 20 | g |  |
| Cream | 300 | ml |  |
| Milk | 200 | ml |  |
| Cheddar | 100 | g | Grated, vegetarian rennet |
| Salt | 0.75 | tsp |  |
| Black pepper |  |  | To taste |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 190°C. Melt the butter in a frying pan over medium heat and cook the leeks for 8 minutes.
2. Layer the potatoes and leeks in a deep baking dish. Season the layers with salt and pepper.
3. Pour over the cream and milk. Cover with foil and bake for 50 minutes.
4. Remove the foil and scatter over the cheese. Bake for 20 more minutes, until the potatoes are tender when pierced.
5. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.
