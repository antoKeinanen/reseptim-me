| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cooked black beans | 480 | g | Drained and rinsed |
| Sweet potato | 500 | g | Peeled and cut into 2 cm cubes |
| Chopped tomatoes | 400 | g | Canned |
| Water | 300 | ml |  |
| Onion | 1 |  | Diced |
| Olive oil | 1 | tbsp |  |
| Ground cumin | 2 | tsp |  |
| Smoked paprika | 1 | tsp |  |
| Salt | 0.5 | tsp |  |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Cook the onion for 5 minutes.
2. Stir in the cumin and paprika for 30 seconds.
3. Add the sweet potato, tomatoes, water, and salt. Simmer with a lid for 20 minutes.
4. Add the beans and simmer uncovered for 10 minutes, until the sweet potato is tender.
