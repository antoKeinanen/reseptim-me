| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cooked cannellini beans | 480 | g | Drained and rinsed |
| Olive oil | 2 | tbsp |  |
| Lemon juice | 2 | tbsp |  |
| Garlic | 1 | clove | Minced |
| Smoked paprika | 1 | tsp |  |
| Water | 3 | tbsp |  |
| Salt | 0.25 | tsp |  |

## Instructions

1. Put the beans, oil, lemon juice, garlic, paprika, water, and salt in a food processor.
2. Blend until smooth. Add another tablespoon of water if the dip is too thick.
3. Transfer to a bowl and chill for 15 minutes before serving.
