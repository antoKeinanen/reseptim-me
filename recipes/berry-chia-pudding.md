| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chia seeds | 80 | g |  |
| Almond milk | 500 | ml |  |
| Maple syrup | 3 | tbsp |  |
| Vanilla extract | 1 | tsp |  |
| Fresh mixed berries | 250 | g | Washed |

## Instructions

1. Whisk the chia seeds, almond milk, maple syrup, and vanilla in a bowl.
2. Leave for 10 minutes, then whisk again to break up any clumps.
3. Divide among four jars. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight.
4. Top with the fresh berries before serving.
