| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cooked beetroot | 400 | g | Cut into wedges |
| Oranges | 2 |  | Peeled and sliced |
| Rocket | 60 | g |  |
| Walnuts | 40 | g | Chopped |
| Olive oil | 2 | tbsp |  |
| Balsamic vinegar | 1 | tbsp |  |
| Salt |  |  | To taste |

## Instructions

1. Arrange the rocket, beetroot, and orange slices on a serving plate.
2. Whisk the oil and vinegar with a pinch of salt.
3. Drizzle the dressing over the salad and scatter over the walnuts.
4. Serve immediately.
