| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Courgettes | 2 |  | About 500 g total |
| Feta | 100 | g | Crumbled, vegetarian rennet |
| Egg | 1 |  |  |
| Plain flour | 60 | g |  |
| Fresh dill | 1 | tbsp | Chopped |
| Olive oil | 2 | tbsp |  |
| Salt | 0.25 | tsp |  |

## Instructions

1. Grate the courgettes and mix with the salt. Leave in a sieve for 10 minutes.
2. Squeeze the courgettes in a clean tea towel to remove excess liquid.
3. Mix the courgettes with the feta, egg, flour, and dill.
4. Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Add spoonfuls of batter and flatten into small fritters.
5. Fry in batches for 3 to 4 minutes on each side, until golden and cooked through.
