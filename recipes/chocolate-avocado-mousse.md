| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ripe avocados | 2 |  | Peeled and stoned |
| Cocoa powder | 30 | g | Unsweetened |
| Maple syrup | 80 | ml |  |
| Almond milk | 60 | ml |  |
| Vanilla extract | 1 | tsp |  |
| Salt | 1 | pinch |  |

## Instructions

1. Put the avocado flesh, cocoa, maple syrup, almond milk, vanilla, and salt in a food processor.
2. Blend until smooth, scraping down the sides as needed.
3. Divide among four small dishes. Cover and chill for at least 1 hour before serving.
