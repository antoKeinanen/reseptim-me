| Couscous | 250 | g |  |
| Boiling water | 300 | ml |  |
| Cooked chickpeas | 400 | g | Drained and rinsed |
| Courgette | 1 |  | Diced |
| Olive oil | 2 | tbsp |  |
| Lemon | 1 |  | Zest and juice |
| Fresh parsley | 20 | g | Chopped |
| Salt | 0.5 | tsp |  |

## Instructions

1. Put the couscous and salt in a bowl. Pour over the boiling water, cover, and leave for 5 minutes.
2. Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Cook the courgette for 8 minutes.
3. Add the chickpeas and cook for 3 minutes, until hot.
4. Fluff the couscous with a fork. Fold in the chickpeas, courgette, lemon zest, lemon juice, and parsley.
