| Vanilla extract | 1 | tsp |  |
| Ground cinnamon |  |  | To serve |

## Instructions

1. Put the rice, milk, sugar, and vanilla in a heavy saucepan.
2. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat, then reduce the heat to low.
3. Cook uncovered for 35 to 40 minutes, stirring often, until the rice is soft and the pudding is creamy.
4. Divide among bowls and dust with cinnamon. Serve warm.
