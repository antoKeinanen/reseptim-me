| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fine polenta | 150 | g |  |
| Ground almonds | 150 | g |  |
| Butter | 150 | g | Softened |
| Caster sugar | 150 | g |  |
| Eggs | 3 |  |  |
| Orange | 1 |  | Zest and juice |
| Baking powder | 1 | tsp | Gluten-free |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 170°C. Line a 20 cm round cake tin with baking paper.
2. Beat the butter and sugar until pale. Beat in the eggs one at a time.
3. Fold in the polenta, almonds, baking powder, orange zest, and orange juice.
4. Transfer to the tin and bake for 40 to 45 minutes, until a skewer inserted in the center comes out clean.
5. Cool in the tin for 15 minutes, then transfer to a rack and cool completely before slicing.
