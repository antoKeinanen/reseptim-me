| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Carrots | 250 | g | Sliced |
| Cooked chickpeas | 240 | g | Drained and rinsed |
| Tahini | 2 | tbsp |  |
| Lemon juice | 2 | tbsp |  |
| Olive oil | 2 | tbsp | Divided |
| Ground cumin | 0.5 | tsp |  |
| Water | 3 | tbsp |  |
| Salt | 0.25 | tsp |  |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Toss the carrots with 1 tablespoon of oil and roast for 25 minutes, until soft.
2. Let the carrots cool for 10 minutes.
3. Blend the carrots, chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice, cumin, water, salt, and remaining oil until smooth.
4. Add a little more water if needed. Spoon into a bowl and serve.
