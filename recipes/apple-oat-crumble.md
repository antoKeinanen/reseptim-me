| Rolled oats | 100 | g |  |
| Plain flour | 100 | g |  |
| Butter | 100 | g | Cold and cubed |
| Brown sugar | 100 | g | Divided |
| Ground cinnamon | 1 | tsp |  |
| Water | 2 | tbsp |  |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Put the apples in a baking dish with the cinnamon, water, and 40 g of sugar.
2. Rub the butter into the flour with your fingertips until the mixture forms coarse crumbs.
3. Stir in the oats and remaining 60 g of sugar. Scatter the topping over the apples.
4. Bake for 40 minutes, until golden and the apples are tender. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.
