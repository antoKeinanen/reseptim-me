| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quinoa | 250 | g | Rinsed |
| Water | 500 | ml |  |
| Carrots | 3 |  | Cut into thin batons |
| Red pepper | 2 |  | Cut into strips |
| Broccoli | 300 | g | Cut into florets |
| Olive oil | 2 | tbsp |  |
| Tahini | 3 | tbsp |  |
| Lemon juice | 2 | tbsp |  |
| Salt | 0.5 | tsp |  |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 210°C. Toss the carrots, peppers, and broccoli with the oil and salt on a baking tray.
2. Roast for 25 minutes, turning once, until tender and browned at the edges.
3. Bring the quinoa and water to a boil in a saucepan. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes, then rest off the heat for 5 minutes.
4. Mix the tahini and lemon juice with 3 tablespoons of water to make a dressing.
5. Divide the quinoa and vegetables among bowls and spoon over the dressing.
