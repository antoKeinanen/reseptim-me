| Ingredient            | Amount | Unit    | Note               |
| --------------------- | ------ | ------- | ------------------ |
| Canned chickpeas      | 400    | g       | Drained and rinsed |
| Cucumber              | 1      |         | Diced              |
| Cherry tomatoes       | 200    | g       | Halved             |
| Small red onion       | 1      |         | Finely chopped     |
| Feta                  | 100    | g       | Crumbled           |
| Fresh parsley         | 1      | handful | Chopped            |
| Olive oil             | 2      | tbsp    |                    |
| Lemon juice           | 1      | tbsp    |                    |
| Salt and black pepper |        |         | To taste           |

Prep time 15 minutes. No cooking required.

## Instructions

1. Put the chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes, and onion in a large bowl.
2. Add the feta and parsley.
3. Whisk the olive oil and lemon juice in a small bowl.
4. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss gently.
5. Season with salt and pepper, then serve.
