| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cucumber | 1 |  |  |
| Greek yogurt | 250 | g |  |
| Garlic | 1 | clove | Minced |
| Lemon juice | 1 | tbsp |  |
| Olive oil | 1 | tbsp |  |
| Fresh dill | 1 | tbsp | Chopped |
| Salt | 0.25 | tsp |  |

## Instructions

1. Grate the cucumber and squeeze out the liquid with a clean tea towel.
2. Mix the cucumber with the yogurt, garlic, lemon juice, oil, dill, and salt.
3. Chill for 20 minutes before serving.
