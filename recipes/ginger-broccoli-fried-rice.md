| Long-grain rice | 300 | g | Uncooked |
| Water | 600 | ml |  |
| Broccoli | 300 | g | Cut into small florets |
| Frozen peas | 150 | g |  |
| Neutral oil | 2 | tbsp |  |
| Fresh ginger | 20 | g | Grated |
| Tamari | 3 | tbsp | Gluten-free |
| Spring onions | 3 |  | Sliced |

## Instructions

1. Bring the rice and water to a boil. Cover, reduce the heat, and cook for 12 minutes or according to the package instructions.
2. Spread the hot rice on a tray and let the steam escape for 10 minutes.
3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Stir-fry the broccoli for 5 minutes.
4. Add the ginger and peas. Cook for 3 minutes.
5. Add the rice and tamari. Stir-fry for 4 minutes, until hot throughout, then scatter over the spring onions.
