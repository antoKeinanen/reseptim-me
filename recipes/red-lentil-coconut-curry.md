| Onion | 1 |  | Diced |
| Olive oil | 1 | tbsp |  |
| Curry powder | 2 | tsp | Gluten-free |
| Salt | 0.5 | tsp |  |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Cook the onion for 5 minutes.
2. Stir in the curry powder and cook for 30 seconds.
3. Add the lentils, coconut milk, water, tomatoes, and salt. Bring to a boil.
4. Reduce the heat and simmer for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the lentils are soft.
