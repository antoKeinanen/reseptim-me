| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chestnut mushrooms | 400 | g | 12 large mushrooms |
| Cream cheese | 150 | g |  |
| Garlic | 2 | cloves | Minced |
| Fresh parsley | 2 | tbsp | Chopped |
| Olive oil | 1 | tbsp |  |
| Black pepper |  |  | To taste |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Remove the mushroom stalks and chop them finely.
2. Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Cook the stalks for 4 minutes, then add the garlic for 1 minute.
3. Mix the cooked stalks with the cream cheese, parsley, and pepper.
4. Put the mushroom caps in a baking dish and fill with the mixture.
5. Bake for 20 minutes, until the mushrooms are tender and the filling is hot.
