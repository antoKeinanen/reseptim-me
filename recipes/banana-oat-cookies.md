| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ripe bananas | 2 |  | Peeled |
| Rolled oats | 150 | g |  |
| Peanut butter | 2 | tbsp |  |
| Dark chocolate chips | 50 | g | Dairy-free |
| Ground cinnamon | 0.5 | tsp |  |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Line a baking tray with baking paper.
2. Mash the bananas in a bowl. Stir in the oats, peanut butter, chocolate chips, and cinnamon.
3. Divide into 12 mounds on the tray and flatten into cookies.
4. Bake for 15 to 18 minutes, until the edges are golden. Cool on the tray for 10 minutes.
