| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aubergines | 2 |  | Cut into 2 cm cubes |
| Cooked butter beans | 480 | g | Drained and rinsed |
| Cherry tomatoes | 400 | g |  |
| Red onion | 1 |  | Cut into wedges |
| Olive oil | 3 | tbsp |  |
| Garlic | 3 | cloves | Minced |
| Dried oregano | 2 | tsp |  |
| Salt | 0.5 | tsp |  |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 210°C. Toss the aubergine, onion, oil, garlic, oregano, and salt in a large roasting dish.
2. Roast for 25 minutes, turning halfway through.
3. Stir in the butter beans and cherry tomatoes.
4. Roast for another 20 minutes, until the aubergine is soft and the tomatoes have burst.
