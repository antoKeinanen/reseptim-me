| Mango flesh | 400 | g | Fresh or thawed frozen |
| Coconut milk | 200 | ml |  |
| Lime juice | 1 | tbsp |  |
| Maple syrup | 2 | tbsp |  |

## Instructions

1. Blend the mango, coconut milk, lime juice, and maple syrup until smooth.
2. Pour into six ice pop molds and insert the sticks.
3. Freeze for at least 6 hours, until solid.
4. Run the molds briefly under warm water to release the ice pops.
