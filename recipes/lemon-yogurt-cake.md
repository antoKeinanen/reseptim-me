| Ingredient | Amount | Unit | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plain flour | 200 | g |  |
| Baking powder | 2 | tsp |  |
| Caster sugar | 150 | g |  |
| Plain yogurt | 150 | g |  |
| Eggs | 2 |  |  |
| Neutral oil | 80 | ml |  |
| Lemon | 1 |  | Zest and juice |

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Line a 20 cm round cake tin with baking paper.
2. Whisk the eggs, sugar, yogurt, oil, lemon zest, and lemon juice in a bowl.
3. Fold in the flour and baking powder until no dry flour remains.
4. Pour into the tin and bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until a skewer inserted in the center comes out clean.
5. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then transfer to a rack.
